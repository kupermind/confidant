Show a lender you control at least X XRP in the pool — without unshielding and without naming your notes. Every verifier node checks the proof read-only; nothing moves, nothing is logged. How it worksThe challenge binds your XRPL account, the lender's id, the current ledger and an expiry. Your wallet builds a proof over it; each node checks it against its own state and answers a verdict. A snapshot, not a lock: you can still spend afterwards — rotate your notes so the nodes cannot link that spend to this proof. The lender id, your account, the amount and the ledger window are seen by the operators of the nodes you ask.

How Confidant worksBalances, explainedTrust model

Notes are non-custodial: this wallet's spending key never reaches the pool. The pool account is held by a k-of-N quorum of independent verifiers; collusion of k can drain the pool account but cannot spend anyone's notes. Shield and unshield amounts and addresses are public by nature; the transfer graph inside the pool is not. Your wallet runs in this browser (WebAssembly): the spending key, your notes and the proofs never leave it. Only if WebAssembly is unavailable does the page fall back to the demo wallet service on the demo host, testnet demo only, never for real value.
