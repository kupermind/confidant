Notes are non-custodial: this wallet's spending key never reaches the pool. The pool account is held by a k-of-N quorum of independent verifiers; collusion of k can drain the pool account but cannot spend anyone's notes. Shield and unshield amounts and addresses are public by nature; the transfer graph inside the pool is not. Your wallet runs in this browser (WebAssembly): the spending key, your notes and the proofs never leave it. Only if WebAssembly is unavailable does the page fall back to the demo wallet service on the demo host — testnet demo only, never for real value.
