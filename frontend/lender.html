Check a borrower's proof of funds

A borrower who holds XRP in the Confidant pool can prove, without unshielding, that they control at least a given amount — and have the pool's signers write that verdict on their XRPL account as an expiring credential. You need only their XRPL address: this page reads the credential from the ledger. No file changes hands.

What the credential tells you — and what it does not

Told. That the account's holder controlled unspent notes in the pool worth at least the amount, as of the ledger named; that each of the pool's signers re-verified the proof on its own node before signing; that the borrower confirmed it with their own signature; and when it expires.

Not told. The balance beyond the amount, which notes, the borrower's history, or anything about other holders. The proof itself never reaches you or the ledger — only this one line does. (The signers' nodes do learn the identifiers of the proven notes; the holder's app replaces those notes right after, which is why this flow is labelled a demo until a circuit that reveals none of them ships.)

Not a lock. The holder can spend right after proving: the credential says "controlled, as of ledger L", not "controls now". Treat an older ledger as older. For collateral that cannot move you need an escrow — a different construction. The expiry is 24 hours from ledger L's close time.
