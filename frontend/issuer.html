Compliance provider

This pool accepts a deposit only from an XRPL address that an authorized compliance provider has verified. The provider is a KYC/AML firm or an accreditation body, not another user of the pool: it checks the holder off-ledger and records the result as an XLS-70 Credential on the XRP Ledger; the holder confirms it with their own signature in the app; every verifier node reads it from the ledger before it signs. The pool never sees documents, and the credential says nothing about anyone's notes. On this testnet the provider is a persona operated by the Confidant team — the persona is a demo; the gate on the ledger is real.

What is checked, by whom, and why

What is checked. One fact only: that this XRPL address belongs to someone an authorized compliance provider has verified. Nothing inside the pool is examined — not your notes, not your balance, not your transfers.

On what basis. The pool operator's policy for regulated use: a pool that institutions may use admits only verified depositors, the same rule a bank or a broker applies when an account is opened. It is not a suspicion about you and not a judgement — the same check applies to every depositor alike, before anything happens.

Who checks. The compliance provider the pool operator trusts: in production a licensed KYC/AML firm or an accreditation body; on this testnet, a demo persona run by the Confidant team. Not the pool, not its verifier nodes, not another user — they only read the result.

What the result is. A yes/no record on the XRP Ledger: an XLS-70 Credential from that provider to your address, which you confirm with your own signature. No name, no document, no personal data is written to the ledger or seen by the pool. The record expires, and the provider can revoke it.

Why. So that a regulated institution can use a shielded pool at all while every depositor keeps their privacy inside it: the entrance is accountable, the graph inside stays hidden. An operator who does not need this runs the pool open — the gate is a per-pool switch, not part of the protocol.
