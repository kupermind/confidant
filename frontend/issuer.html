Demo issuer: a KYC provider persona

The pool can be told to accept deposits only from XRPL accounts that hold an accepted XLS-70 Credential from a trusted issuer. This page is that issuer, for the demo: an ordinary testnet account that signs CredentialCreate for an address you paste in. The holder then accepts it in the app (CredentialAccept, signed by their own account), and only then does the pool count it. The persona is a demo; the gate on the ledger is real.

What this proves on stage: eligibility is an on-ledger fact anyone can check (account_objects, type credential), issued by a party the pool trusts and accepted by the holder's own signature; the pool's verifiers read it from the ledger themselves and refuse a deposit without it. What it does not prove: anything about the issuer's diligence — a demo persona vouches for whoever you paste in.

This page holds the demo issuer's seed in this browser's storage (testnet only, never real value) and talks to the public XRPL WebSocket. It never touches the pool or anyone's notes. ?demo=1 simulates everything.
